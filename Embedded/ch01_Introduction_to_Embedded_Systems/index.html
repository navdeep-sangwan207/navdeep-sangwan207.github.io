Chapter 01: Introduction to Embedded Systems

Understand the fundamentals of embedded systems, their characteristics, classifications, real-time operation, applications, building blocks, design process, and development lifecycle.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the meaning and purpose of embedded systems.
  • Identify the important characteristics of embedded systems.
  • Differentiate embedded systems from general-purpose computers.
  • Classify embedded systems based on functionality and performance.
  • Understand hard real-time and soft real-time systems.
  • Explore embedded systems used in industrial and consumer applications.
  • Identify the basic hardware and software building blocks.
  • Understand the embedded system design process.
  • Learn the stages of the embedded system development lifecycle.

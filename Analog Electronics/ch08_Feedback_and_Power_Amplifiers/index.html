Chapter 8: Feedback and Power Amplifiers

Analog Electronics | Placement-Oriented Learning

Feedback Theory • Amplifier Topologies • Power Stages • Thermal Design

Chapter Overview

This chapter develops the theory and practical design of feedback amplifiers and power amplifiers. It covers feedback types, amplifier stability, gain and bandwidth, Class A/B/AB/C operation, push-pull circuits, efficiency, thermal management and fault diagnosis.

Learning Outcomes

  • Analyze positive and negative feedback systems.
  • Calculate closed-loop gain, sensitivity and bandwidth changes.
  • Identify voltage-series, voltage-shunt, current-series and current-shunt feedback.
  • Understand Class A, B, AB and C power amplifier operation.
  • Analyze crossover distortion and push-pull output stages.
  • Calculate power output, efficiency, transistor dissipation and heatsink requirements.
  • Design and troubleshoot practical low-voltage amplifier stages.

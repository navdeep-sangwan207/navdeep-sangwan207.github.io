Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces operational amplifiers (Op-Amps), their internal architecture, characteristics, configurations and practical applications. It covers IC 741, inverting and non-inverting amplifiers, voltage followers, summing circuits, differential amplifiers, integrators and differentiators.

Practical examples, circuit diagrams, numerical problems, troubleshooting methods and interview questions are included to support electronics and embedded systems placement preparation.

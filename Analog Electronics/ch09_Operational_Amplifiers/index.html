Chapter Overview

An operational amplifier (op-amp) is a high-gain differential voltage amplifier widely used in analog signal processing, amplification, filtering, instrumentation, control systems and measurement circuits.

This chapter develops op-amp concepts from the internal functional characteristics to practical amplifier configurations and circuit calculations.

Analog Circuit Design Op-Amp Applications Circuit Analysis Numerical Problems Placement Preparation

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the op-amp block diagram, terminals and pin configuration.
  • Compare ideal and practical operational amplifier characteristics.
  • Interpret important op-amp data-sheet parameters.
  • Analyze open-loop operation and comparator behavior.
  • Calculate gain in inverting and non-inverting amplifier circuits.
  • Understand voltage followers and buffer applications.
  • Analyze differential amplifiers and common-mode rejection.
  • Identify input common-mode, output swing and frequency limitations.
  • Solve op-amp numerical problems and practical circuit-analysis questions.

Chapter Lecture Sequence

Complete the lectures in the following sequence. Each lecture contains explanations, circuit diagrams, mathematical derivations, interactive demonstrations, practical examples and placement-oriented questions.

L01 – Operational Amplifier Fundamentals and Pin Configuration Definition, internal block diagram, differential inputs, output terminal, supply pins and IC pin identification. L02 – Ideal and Practical Op-Amp Characteristics Ideal assumptions, practical behavior, gain, input/output impedance, bandwidth and comparison. L03 – Op-Amp Parameters and Data Sheet Specifications Input offset, bias current, slew rate, CMRR, PSRR, gain-bandwidth product and data-sheet interpretation. L04 – Open-Loop Op-Amp Operation Open-loop gain, saturation, differential input, comparator operation and transfer characteristics. L05 – Inverting Amplifier Circuit and Gain Virtual ground, feedback operation, gain derivation, input/output waveforms and design calculations. L06 – Non-Inverting Amplifier Circuit and Gain Non-inverting configuration, feedback network, voltage gain, input impedance and circuit design. L07 – Voltage Follower and Buffer Circuit Unity-gain operation, impedance isolation, loading effects and buffer applications. L08 – Differential Amplifier and CMRR Differential gain, common-mode gain, resistor matching, CMRR and instrumentation applications. L09 – Op-Amp Input and Output Limitations Input common-mode range, output swing, output current, slew rate, bandwidth and saturation recovery. L10 – Op-Amp Numerical Problems and Circuit Analysis Gain calculations, summing circuits, feedback analysis, practical limitations and placement problems.

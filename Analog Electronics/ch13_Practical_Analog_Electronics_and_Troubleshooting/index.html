Chapter 13: Practical Analog Electronics and Troubleshooting

Hands-on Electronics Testing, Measurement, Circuit Construction, Fault Diagnosis and Simulation

Analog Electronics — Placement Preparation Course

Chapter Overview

This chapter develops practical skills required in electronics testing, hardware development, electronics manufacturing, maintenance, service engineering and R&D roles. It covers component identification, datasheet interpretation, multimeter testing, circuit construction, measurement instruments, troubleshooting, PCB rework and circuit simulation.

Learning Objectives

  • Read electronic component datasheets and identify important specifications.
  • Identify passive and active electronic components.
  • Use a digital multimeter for voltage, current, resistance and continuity measurements.
  • Test diodes, Zener diodes, BJTs and MOSFETs.
  • Build and analyze transistor, relay and LED driver circuits.
  • Test rectifiers, filters and regulated DC power supplies.
  • Test operational amplifier circuits and diagnose common faults.
  • Use an oscilloscope and function generator for signal analysis.
  • Develop a systematic circuit fault-finding procedure.
  • Understand PCB inspection, soldering and component rework.
  • Simulate analog circuits using Proteus and LTspice.

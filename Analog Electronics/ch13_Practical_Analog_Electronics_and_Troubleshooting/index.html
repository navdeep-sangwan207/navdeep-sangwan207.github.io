Chapter 13: Practical Analog Electronics and Troubleshooting

Practical Electronics, Component Testing, Circuit Analysis and Troubleshooting

Chapter Overview

This chapter focuses on practical analog electronics skills required in electronics testing, hardware development, maintenance and troubleshooting. It covers electronic component identification, multimeter testing, circuit measurements, PCB rework and circuit simulation using Proteus and LTspice.

Chapter 13 Lessons

L01

Reading Electronic Component Datasheets

Understand datasheet specifications, pin configurations, ratings and electrical characteristics.

 L02

Electronic Components Identification and Digital Multimeter Basics

Identify common electronic components and learn voltage, current, resistance and continuity measurements.

 L03

Diode and Zener Diode Testing Using a Multimeter

Test diode forward voltage, reverse blocking and Zener diode characteristics.

 L04

BJT Testing Using a Multimeter

Identify transistor terminals and test NPN and PNP transistors.

 L05

MOSFET Testing Using a Multimeter

Identify MOSFET terminals and perform basic N-channel and P-channel MOSFET tests.

 L06

Transistor Switching Circuit

Understand transistor operation as an electronic switch and calculate base resistor values.

 L07

Relay Driver Circuit

Design and understand transistor-based relay drivers, flyback diodes and load switching.

 L08

LED Driver Circuit

Understand LED current limiting, resistor calculations and transistor-based LED drivers.

 L09

Rectifier and Filter Circuit Testing

Test half-wave, full-wave and bridge rectifiers, filter capacitors and output ripple.

 L10

Regulated Power Supply Testing and Troubleshooting

Measure regulated outputs and diagnose common power supply faults.

 L11

Operational Amplifier Circuit Testing

Test op-amp circuits including amplifiers, comparators and signal conditioning circuits.

 L12

Oscilloscope Basics and Waveform Measurement

Measure amplitude, frequency, time period, phase and waveform characteristics.

 L13

Function Generator Basics and Signal Testing

Generate sine, square and triangular signals and test analog circuits.

 L14

Circuit Fault Finding and Troubleshooting

Develop systematic fault diagnosis skills using measurements and circuit analysis.

 L15

PCB Testing, Soldering and Rework Fundamentals

Learn PCB inspection, soldering, desoldering, continuity testing and component replacement.

 L16

Analog Circuit Simulation Using Proteus

Build and simulate analog circuits using virtual instruments and component models.

 L17

Analog Circuit Simulation Using LTspice

Perform transient, DC operating point and AC analysis of analog electronic circuits.

Chapter 3: Semiconductor Diodes and Applications

Analog Electronics | Placement-Oriented Learning

Chapter Overview

This chapter covers the working principles, electrical characteristics, mathematical analysis and practical applications of semiconductor diodes. It includes circuit calculations, waveform analysis, switching applications, protection circuits and troubleshooting techniques used in electronics engineering.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand PN junction formation and diode operation.
  • Analyze forward-biased and reverse-biased diode circuits.
  • Interpret diode V-I characteristic curves.
  • Calculate diode current, voltage and power dissipation.
  • Understand the diode equation and operating parameters.
  • Explain Zener and avalanche breakdown mechanisms.
  • Analyze Zener voltage regulator circuits.
  • Understand special-purpose semiconductor diodes.
  • Analyze diode clipping and clamping circuits.
  • Apply diodes in switching and circuit protection.
  • Perform practical diode testing and troubleshooting.
  • Prepare for electronics and embedded systems interviews.

Chapter 3 Lectures

01
Introduction to PN Junction Diode

Diode construction, PN junction formation, symbol, working principle and fundamental diode operation.

02
Forward and Reverse Biasing

Forward bias, reverse bias, depletion region, current flow and diode circuit behavior.

03
Diode V-I Characteristics

Forward and reverse characteristics, characteristic graphs, knee voltage, leakage current and curve interpretation.

04
Diode Equation and Operating Parameters

Shockley diode equation, thermal voltage, ideality factor, current calculations and operating parameters.

05
Diode Breakdown Mechanisms

Zener breakdown, avalanche breakdown, reverse breakdown voltage and limitations.

06
Zener Diode and Voltage Regulation

Zener diode operation, regulator circuits, load regulation, line regulation and design calculations.

07
Special Purpose Semiconductor Diodes

LED, photodiode, Schottky diode, PIN diode, varactor diode and their applications.

08
Diode Clippers and Clampers

Positive and negative clippers, biased clipping, clamping circuits and waveform analysis.

09
Diode Switching and Protection Circuits

Switching behavior, reverse recovery, flyback diodes, reverse polarity protection and transient protection applications.

10
Practical Diode Circuits and Troubleshooting

Multimeter testing, circuit measurements, fault diagnosis, practical experiments and interview preparation.

Placement Preparation

After completing this chapter, you should be able to:

  • Explain the working principle of a PN junction diode.
  • Differentiate forward bias and reverse bias.
  • Draw and explain diode V-I characteristics.
  • Solve diode circuit numerical problems.
  • Explain Zener and avalanche breakdown.
  • Design basic Zener voltage regulation circuits.
  • Explain LED, photodiode and Schottky diode applications.
  • Analyze clipping and clamping circuits.
  • Explain flyback diode operation in inductive circuits.
  • Test a diode using a digital multimeter.
  • Diagnose basic semiconductor circuit faults.

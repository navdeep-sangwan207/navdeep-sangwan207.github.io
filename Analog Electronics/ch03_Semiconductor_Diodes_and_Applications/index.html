Chapter Overview

Semiconductor diodes are fundamental electronic components that allow current to flow preferentially in one direction. They are widely used in rectifiers, switching circuits, voltage regulation, protection circuits and signal-processing applications.

This chapter explains PN junction diode operation, forward and reverse bias, V-I characteristics, diode equations, breakdown mechanisms, Zener diodes and practical diode circuits.

Learning Outcomes

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

  • Explain the working principle of a PN junction diode.
  • Understand forward and reverse bias operation.
  • Interpret diode V-I characteristics.
  • Understand the diode current equation.
  • Calculate approximate diode current and voltage.
  • Differentiate Zener and avalanche breakdown.
  • Explain Zener diode voltage regulation.
  • Understand special-purpose semiconductor diodes.
  • Analyse clipping and clamping circuits.
  • Identify diode applications in embedded and electronic systems.

Placement Preparation Focus

  • Explain forward and reverse bias with diagrams.
  • Draw and interpret the diode V-I characteristics.
  • Calculate current using the diode equation.
  • Differentiate Zener and avalanche breakdown.
  • Calculate the series resistor for a Zener regulator.
  • Explain flyback diode operation in relay and motor circuits.
  • Test a diode using a digital multimeter.
Practical Placement Tip: Pay particular attention to diode testing, Zener voltage regulation, reverse polarity protection and flyback diode applications. These concepts are useful in electronics testing and embedded hardware interviews.

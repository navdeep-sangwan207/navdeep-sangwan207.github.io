Chapter 3: Semiconductor Diodes and Applications

Analog Electronics | NS Learning Hub

Chapter Overview

This chapter develops the practical understanding of semiconductor diodes, their electrical characteristics, circuit analysis, application circuits, component selection, testing, and troubleshooting.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand diode construction, symbols and working principles.
  • Analyze forward-bias and reverse-bias operation.
  • Understand diode I-V characteristics.
  • Analyze diode circuits using different models.
  • Calculate diode current, voltage and power dissipation.
  • Understand diode switching characteristics.
  • Analyze clipping and clamping circuits.
  • Understand special-purpose semiconductor diodes.
  • Perform practical diode testing and troubleshooting.
  • Prepare for electronics and embedded systems interviews.

  • KCL and KVL.
  • Series and parallel circuits.
  • Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductor concepts.
  • Majority and minority carriers.
  • PN junction and depletion region.

  • Temperature effects on semiconductors.

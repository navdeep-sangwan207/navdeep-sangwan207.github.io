Course Overview

This course develops the circuit-analysis knowledge and practical electronics skills required for technical interviews and entry-level industry roles. It covers semiconductor devices, transistor circuits, amplifiers, operational amplifiers, power supplies, signal generation, circuit protection and practical troubleshooting.

13 Chapters
142 Lectures
100% Placement-Oriented Focus

Course Objectives

  • Understand the operation of electronic components and semiconductor devices.
  • Analyze diode, BJT, MOSFET and operational amplifier circuits.
  • Solve numerical problems involving voltage, current, gain, power and frequency.
  • Design and understand practical analog circuits.
  • Read component datasheets and select suitable electronic components.
  • Perform circuit testing, measurements and fault diagnosis.
  • Prepare for technical interviews and electronics placement assessments.

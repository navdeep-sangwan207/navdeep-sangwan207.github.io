What You Will Learn

  • Understand semiconductor materials and PN junction devices.
  • Analyze diode characteristics and practical diode circuits.
  • Design and understand rectifiers, filters and regulated power supplies.
  • Understand BJT configurations, biasing and transistor amplifiers.
  • Analyze JFET, MOSFET and power MOSFET circuits.
  • Understand feedback and power amplifier operation.
  • Design and analyze operational amplifier circuits.
  • Understand comparators, Schmitt triggers and active filters.
  • Study oscillator circuits and 555 timer applications.
  • Understand voltage regulation and electronic circuit protection.
  • Read semiconductor component datasheets.
  • Use multimeters, oscilloscopes and signal generators.
  • Perform analog circuit testing and troubleshooting.

Prerequisites

  • Basic knowledge of electrical circuits.
  • Understanding of voltage, current, resistance and power.
  • Basic knowledge of resistors, capacitors and inductors.
  • Understanding of Ohm's Law and Kirchhoff's Laws.
  • Basic mathematics and circuit analysis skills.

Recommended Tools and Software

  • Digital Multimeter (DMM).
  • Digital Storage Oscilloscope (DSO).
  • Function or Signal Generator.
  • DC Regulated Power Supply.
  • Breadboard and electronic components.
  • Proteus Design Suite.
  • LTspice Circuit Simulator.
  • Semiconductor component datasheets.

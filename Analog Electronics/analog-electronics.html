Course Overview

This course develops the technical knowledge and practical problem-solving skills required for Electronics and Communication, Embedded Systems, Hardware Testing and Electronics R&D placements.

  • Semiconductor device operation and characteristics.
  • Diode, transistor and MOSFET circuit analysis.
  • Biasing, amplifiers and operational amplifier applications.
  • Rectifiers, filters, voltage regulators and protection circuits.
  • Numerical problem-solving and technical interview preparation.
  • Component testing, circuit simulation and fault diagnosis.

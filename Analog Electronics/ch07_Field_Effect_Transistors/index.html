Chapter 7: Field Effect Transistors (FETs and MOSFETs)

Study JFETs, MOSFETs, device characteristics, operating regions, biasing, switching circuits, power MOSFETs and practical troubleshooting techniques.

Chapter Overview

Field Effect Transistors are voltage-controlled semiconductor devices in which an electric field controls the current flowing through a channel. They are widely used in analog amplifiers, electronic switching, power converters and embedded hardware.

This chapter covers the fundamental operation of JFETs and MOSFETs, their characteristics, biasing techniques and practical applications relevant to electronics and embedded systems.

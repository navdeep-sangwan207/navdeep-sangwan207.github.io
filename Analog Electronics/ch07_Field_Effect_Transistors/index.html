Chapter Overview

Field Effect Transistors are voltage-controlled semiconductor devices in which an electric field controls the current flowing through a semiconductor channel. FETs are widely used in analog amplifiers, electronic switching, power conversion, embedded systems, signal conditioning and digital electronics.

  • Calculate MOSFET operating points and switching parameters.

Placement Preparation Focus

  • Explain why a FET is called a voltage-controlled device.
  • Differentiate JFET pinch-off voltage and MOSFET threshold voltage.
  • Calculate JFET drain current using the Shockley equation.
  • Determine the Q-point of a self-biased JFET.
  • Explain MOSFET cutoff, triode/ohmic and saturation regions.
  • Calculate MOSFET gate-drive and load-switching requirements.
  • Explain why MOSFET gate charge matters in switching applications.
  • Select a logic-level MOSFET for a microcontroller-controlled load.
  • Explain flyback protection when switching an inductive load.
  • Diagnose a MOSFET that overheats or fails to switch fully ON.

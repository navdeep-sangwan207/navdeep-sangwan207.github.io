Chapter Overview

This chapter covers practical operational amplifier applications used in electronics design, signal processing, measurement circuits, sensor interfacing, control systems and electronic instrumentation.

Placement preparation focus: Circuit analysis, transfer functions, design calculations, circuit behavior, component selection, practical limitations, circuit debugging and interview preparation.

Learning Outcomes

  • Design summing and weighted-addition circuits.
  • Analyze subtractors and differential amplifier applications.
  • Understand op-amp integrator and differentiator circuits.
  • Design comparator and zero-crossing detector circuits.
  • Understand Schmitt triggers and hysteresis.
  • Analyze precision rectifiers and peak detectors.
  • Design active low-pass, high-pass, band-pass and notch filters.
  • Understand instrumentation amplifiers.
  • Design and debug practical op-amp application circuits.
  • Analyze voltage-to-current and current-to-voltage converters.

Chapter 10: Op-Amp Applications

Analog Electronics

Operational Amplifier Circuits and Signal Processing

Chapter Overview

This chapter covers important practical applications of operational amplifiers, including mathematical circuits, comparators, signal detectors, precision rectifiers, active filters and voltage/current converters.

Emphasis is placed on circuit operation, design equations, practical applications and placement-oriented concepts.

Total Lessons: 15

Learning Objectives

  • Understand op-amp integrator and differentiator circuits.
  • Analyze comparator and voltage detection circuits.
  • Understand Schmitt trigger and window comparator operation.
  • Study precision rectifier circuits.
  • Analyze active filter circuits and frequency response.
  • Understand voltage-to-current and current-to-voltage converters.
  • Apply op-amp concepts to practical electronic systems.

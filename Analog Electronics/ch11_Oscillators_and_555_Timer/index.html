Chapter Overview

This chapter explains electronic oscillators and timer circuits used to generate periodic waveforms, timing pulses and switching signals. It begins with oscillator fundamentals and the Barkhausen criterion, progresses through RC, LC and crystal oscillators, and concludes with practical applications of the 555 timer IC.

Total Lectures: 10

Learning Level: Basic to Advanced | Placement-Oriented

Practical Focus: Frequency calculation, oscillator design, waveform analysis, timer circuits and troubleshooting.

0 of 10 lectures completed

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the principle of sustained oscillations.
  • Apply the Barkhausen criterion to oscillator circuits.
  • Analyze RC phase-shift and Wien bridge oscillators.
  • Understand Colpitts and Hartley LC oscillator circuits.
  • Explain crystal oscillator operation and frequency stability.
  • Understand the internal architecture and pin configuration of the 555 timer.
  • Design monostable and astable timer circuits.
  • Analyze bistable operation and 555 Schmitt trigger configurations.
  • Calculate oscillator frequency, time period and pulse width.
  • Build, test and troubleshoot practical oscillator and timer circuits.

Chapter Overview

This chapter explains how a Bipolar Junction Transistor is biased to establish a stable operating point and how it is used as a small-signal amplifier. It progresses from basic biasing circuits to practical amplifier analysis and design.

Chapter Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the need for transistor biasing in amplifier circuits.
  • Calculate the DC operating point of different BJT biasing circuits.
  • Analyse fixed-bias, collector-to-base bias and voltage-divider bias.
  • Understand emitter stabilization and thermal stability.
  • Draw and analyse DC load lines and determine the Q-point.
  • Understand small-signal transistor amplifier operation.
  • Analyse common-emitter, common-collector and common-base amplifiers.
  • Calculate voltage gain, current gain and power gain.
  • Determine input and output impedance.
  • Solve numerical problems relevant to electronics interviews and technical assessments.

Chapter 5: Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)

Understand transistor construction, operation, configurations, characteristics, switching, amplification and biasing techniques.

Chapter Overview

A Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) is a three-terminal semiconductor device widely used for electronic switching and signal amplification.

This chapter covers NPN and PNP transistors, transistor configurations, current relationships, operating regions, switching applications, amplifier fundamentals and biasing.

Placement Focus: BJT operating regions, CE configuration, current gain, transistor switching, biasing, load-line analysis and practical circuit troubleshooting.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand BJT construction and working principle.
  • Differentiate between NPN and PNP transistors.
  • Identify cutoff, active and saturation regions.
  • Understand CB, CE and CC configurations.
  • Calculate base, collector and emitter currents.
  • Understand transistor current gains α, β and γ.
  • Use a BJT as an electronic switch.
  • Understand basic transistor amplification.
  • Analyze transistor biasing and DC load lines.
  • Troubleshoot basic transistor circuits.

Chapter Learning Outcomes

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

  • Differentiate conductors, insulators and semiconductors.
  • Explain atomic structure and covalent bonding.
  • Interpret semiconductor energy band diagrams.
  • Explain electron-hole pair generation and recombination.
  • Understand intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors.
  • Differentiate P-type and N-type materials.
  • Explain drift and diffusion current mechanisms.
  • Describe the formation of a PN junction and depletion region.

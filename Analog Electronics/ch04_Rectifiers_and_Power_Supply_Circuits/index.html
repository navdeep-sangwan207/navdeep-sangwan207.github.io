Chapter 4: Rectifiers and Power Supply Circuits

Understand AC-to-DC conversion, rectifier circuits, filtering techniques and regulated DC power supplies.

Placement-Oriented Analog Electronics

Chapter Overview

Rectifiers are fundamental electronic circuits used to convert alternating current (AC) into direct current (DC). They are widely used in power adapters, battery chargers, DC power supplies and electronic equipment.

This chapter explains half-wave, full-wave and bridge rectifiers, their performance parameters, filter circuits and voltage regulation techniques.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the working principle of rectifier circuits.
  • Analyze half-wave and full-wave rectifiers.
  • Understand centre-tapped and bridge rectifier configurations.
  • Calculate average DC output and RMS output voltage.
  • Understand ripple factor and rectification efficiency.
  • Analyze capacitor, LC and π filter circuits.
  • Understand voltage regulation and load regulation.
  • Design and troubleshoot practical DC power supplies.

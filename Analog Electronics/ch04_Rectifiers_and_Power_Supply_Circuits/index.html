Analog Electronics | NS Learning Hub

AC-to-DC Conversion, Rectification, Filtering and DC Supply Design

Chapter Overview

This chapter explains how alternating current (AC) is converted into direct current (DC) using semiconductor rectifiers. It covers half-wave, centre-tapped and bridge rectifiers, rectifier performance, filter circuits, transformer selection and practical DC power supply design.

Emphasis is placed on circuit analysis, waveform interpretation, component selection, numerical calculations, practical measurements and electronics troubleshooting.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand AC-to-DC conversion and rectifier operation.
  • Analyze half-wave and full-wave rectifier circuits.
  • Calculate PIV, average output and RMS output voltage.
  • Determine rectifier efficiency and ripple factor.
  • Understand capacitor, LC and π filter circuits.
  • Select suitable transformers and rectifier components.
  • Design and calculate practical DC power supplies.
  • Diagnose common rectifier and power supply faults.

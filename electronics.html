Projects and Course Contents

1. Basic Electronic Components

Resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors and integrated circuits.

2. Circuit Analysis

Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, voltage, current, power and equivalent circuits.

3. Rectifier Circuits

Half-wave, full-wave and bridge rectifiers, ripple and filtering.

4. Transistor Switching

BJT and MOSFET switching, biasing and basic driver circuits.

5. Op-Amp and Comparator Circuits

Voltage comparison, threshold detection and signal conditioning.

6. Under- and Overvoltage Protection

Comparator-based voltage monitoring, adjustable thresholds and indicator LEDs.

7. Power Supply Design

AC-to-DC conversion, filtering, regulation and protection.

8. Oscillators and Signal Circuits

Waveform generation, timing circuits and signal conditioning.

9. Circuit Simulation

Use LTspice to draw schematics, run simulations and examine waveforms.

10. PCB Design Fundamentals

Schematic capture, component footprints, routing and design-rule checks.

11. Testing and Troubleshooting

Multimeter measurements, fault isolation and comparison with expected results.

12. Project Documentation

Objectives, circuit diagrams, component lists, test results and conclusions.

Chapter 1: Introduction to STM32 and Embedded Systems

Understanding STM32 Microcontrollers, Applications and Development Platforms

Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces STM32 microcontrollers and their role in modern embedded systems. You will learn about STM32 product families, applications, development tools and commonly used development boards.

It establishes the foundation for programming STM32 devices using Embedded C, STM32CubeIDE, STM32CubeMX and ARM Cortex-M architecture.

Learning Outcomes

  • Define a microcontroller and explain its role in embedded systems.
  • Understand the basic features of STM32 devices.
  • Differentiate major STM32 product families.
  • Identify common industrial and consumer applications.
  • Understand the STM32 development ecosystem.
  • Identify the main components of an STM32 Nucleo board.

Placement Preparation

After completing this chapter, prepare to answer questions such as:

  • What is STM32?
  • What is the difference between a microcontroller and a microprocessor?
  • What does ARM Cortex-M mean?
  • What are the major STM32 product families?
  • What is STM32CubeIDE?
  • What is the purpose of an STM32 Nucleo board?

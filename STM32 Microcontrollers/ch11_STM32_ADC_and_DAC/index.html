Chapter Overview

ADC and DAC peripherals allow STM32 microcontrollers to interact with analog signals. ADC converts analog voltage into digital values, while DAC generates analog output from digital data.

Practical Skills

  • Calculate voltage from ADC digital output.
  • Interface a potentiometer with an ADC input.

  • Debug ADC conversion and analog signal problems.

Placement Preparation

Focus on ADC conversion calculations, sampling time, resolution, reference voltage, HAL ADC functions, DMA operation and practical sensor interfacing.

Be prepared to explain how an STM32 reads an analog sensor and converts the digital result into a voltage measurement.

Chapter Overview

The clock system determines the operating frequency of the STM32 processor and its peripherals. Understanding clock configuration is essential for accurate timer operation, UART baud rates, ADC conversion and real-time embedded applications.

Chapter Learning Objectives

  • Understand the STM32 clock tree architecture.
  • Identify internal and external clock sources.
  • Understand HSI, HSE, LSI and LSE oscillators.
  • Configure PLL for the required system frequency.
  • Understand AHB and APB bus clocks.
  • Calculate peripheral clock frequencies.
  • Understand clock prescalers and clock distribution.
  • Identify different STM32 reset sources.
  • Understand system clock initialization.

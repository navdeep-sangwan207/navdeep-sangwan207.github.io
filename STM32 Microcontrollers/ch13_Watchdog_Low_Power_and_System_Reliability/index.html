Chapter Overview

System reliability is essential in embedded applications. Microcontrollers may experience software lockups, unexpected resets, power interruptions and peripheral failures.

STM32 provides watchdog timers, low-power modes, reset information and fault-handling mechanisms to help developers build reliable firmware.

This chapter focuses on practical concepts frequently discussed in embedded firmware interviews.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand Independent Watchdog operation.
  • Configure the Window Watchdog.
  • Differentiate IWDG and WWDG.
  • Understand Sleep, Stop and Standby modes.
  • Configure and identify wakeup sources.
  • Understand reset causes and reset flags.
  • Analyze HardFault and common firmware failures.
  • Implement error handling and recovery mechanisms.
  • Understand fail-safe firmware design.

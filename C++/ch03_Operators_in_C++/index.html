About This Chapter

Operators are special symbols used to perform operations on variables and values in C++. They are essential for calculations, comparisons, logical decisions, bit manipulation and assignment operations.

In this chapter, you will learn different categories of C++ operators, namespaces, the scope resolution operator, and the rules of operator precedence and associativity.

Learning Objectives

After completing this chapter, you should be able to:

  • Identify and use different categories of C++ operators.
  • Perform arithmetic and comparison operations.
  • Use logical operators in conditional expressions.
  • Perform bitwise and bit-shift operations.
  • Understand assignment and conditional operators.
  • Use namespaces and the scope resolution operator.
  • Evaluate expressions using precedence and associativity rules.

Chapter 2: AUTOSAR Classic Platform Architecture

Placement-Oriented AUTOSAR Course

6 Important Lessons

Chapter Overview

This chapter explains the architecture of the AUTOSAR Classic Platform, which is widely used in automotive embedded systems requiring predictable real-time behavior.

You will study the Application Layer, Runtime Environment (RTE), Basic Software (BSW), Microcontroller Abstraction Layer (MCAL), and ECU Abstraction Layer.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the three-layer AUTOSAR Classic architecture.
  • Explain the role of the Application Layer and SWCs.
  • Understand RTE communication and generated interfaces.
  • Identify important BSW modules and their functions.
  • Understand MCAL drivers and microcontroller peripherals.
  • Differentiate ECU Abstraction from MCAL.

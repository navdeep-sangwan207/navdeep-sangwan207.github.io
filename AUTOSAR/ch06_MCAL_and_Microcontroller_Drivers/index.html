Chapter 6: MCAL and Microcontroller Drivers

Understand the AUTOSAR Microcontroller Abstraction Layer, peripheral drivers and hardware-independent access to microcontroller peripherals.

Total Lessons: 6

Focus: Automotive Embedded Systems Placement Preparation

Chapter Overview

The Microcontroller Abstraction Layer (MCAL) is a part of AUTOSAR Basic Software. It provides standardized interfaces to microcontroller peripherals and helps isolate upper software layers from microcontroller-specific hardware details.

This chapter focuses on important peripheral drivers such as Port, DIO, ADC, PWM, GPT, SPI, I2C and CAN, along with MCAL configuration and integration.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand MCAL architecture and responsibilities.
  • Understand Port and DIO driver functions.
  • Learn ADC, PWM and GPT driver concepts.
  • Understand SPI and I2C driver concepts.
  • Learn CAN driver and CAN transceiver roles.
  • Understand MCAL configuration and integration.
  • Differentiate MCAL drivers from ECU Abstraction modules.

Chapter 1: Introduction to AUTOSAR

Automotive Open System Architecture

5 Placement-Oriented Lessons

Chapter Overview

This chapter introduces AUTOSAR, its objectives, standardization, development partnership and application in automotive embedded systems. You will understand why automotive manufacturers and suppliers use AUTOSAR to develop scalable, reusable and interoperable ECU software.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand AUTOSAR fundamentals and terminology.
  • Identify the need for AUTOSAR in automotive development.
  • Understand AUTOSAR standards and partnership.
  • Differentiate Classic and Adaptive AUTOSAR.
  • Understand the basic architecture of automotive ECU software.

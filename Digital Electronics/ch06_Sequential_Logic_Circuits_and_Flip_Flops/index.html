Chapter 6: Sequential Logic Circuits and Flip-Flops

Digital Electronics – Placement-Oriented Course

Chapter Overview

Sequential logic circuits are digital circuits whose outputs depend on both present inputs and previously stored information. They use memory elements such as latches and flip-flops to store binary data.

This chapter covers the operation, truth tables, characteristic equations and excitation tables of commonly used flip-flops.

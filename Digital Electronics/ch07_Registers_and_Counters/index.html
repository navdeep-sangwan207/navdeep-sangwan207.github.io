Chapter Overview

This chapter covers registers, shift registers and digital counters used in sequential logic circuits. You will learn how digital data is stored, transferred and shifted using flip-flops.

It also explains asynchronous and synchronous counters, binary up-down counting and MOD-N counter design. These concepts are important for digital hardware, microcontrollers, processors and embedded systems.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the structure and operation of digital registers.
  • Differentiate between SISO, SIPO, PISO and PIPO registers.
  • Understand serial and parallel data transfer.
  • Analyze asynchronous ripple counters.
  • Design and understand synchronous counters.
  • Differentiate between up, down and up-down counters.
  • Calculate the number of flip-flops required for MOD-N counters.
  • Understand counter applications in embedded and digital systems.

Placement and Interview Relevance

Registers and counters are frequently encountered in digital electronics, embedded systems and hardware design interviews.

Pay particular attention to shift-register operation, counter timing, flip-flop connections, frequency division and MOD-N counter design.

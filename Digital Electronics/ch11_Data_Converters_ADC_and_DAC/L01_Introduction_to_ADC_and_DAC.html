Chapter Overview

Real-world signals such as temperature, pressure, sound and voltage are generally analog in nature. Digital systems and microcontrollers process information in digital form.

Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADC) convert analog signals into digital values, while Digital-to-Analog Converters (DAC) convert digital data into corresponding analog signals.

This chapter introduces important ADC and DAC architectures and explains their working principles, characteristics and applications.

Chapter Overview

Real-world signals such as temperature, pressure, sound, light and voltage are generally analog in nature. Digital systems and microcontrollers process information in digital form.

Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADCs) convert analog signals into digital values, while Digital-to-Analog Converters (DACs) convert digital data into corresponding analog voltage or current signals.

This chapter explains the working principles, architectures and practical applications of commonly used ADC and DAC circuits in embedded systems and electronic devices.

Chapter 4: Combinational Logic Circuits

Digital Electronics – Placement-Oriented Course

Chapter Overview

This chapter covers combinational logic circuits used in digital systems. You will learn how to design and understand adders, subtractors, multiplexers, demultiplexers, encoders and decoders.

The focus is on Boolean expressions, truth tables, logic diagrams, circuit operation and practical applications commonly discussed in electronics and embedded systems interviews.

Chapter Learning Outcomes

After completing this chapter, you will be able to:

  • Explain the characteristics of combinational circuits.
  • Design half adders and full adders.
  • Understand half subtractor and full subtractor circuits.
  • Derive Boolean expressions from truth tables.
  • Explain multiplexer and demultiplexer operation.
  • Understand encoder and decoder circuits.
  • Identify common digital IC applications.
  • Answer placement interview questions on combinational logic.

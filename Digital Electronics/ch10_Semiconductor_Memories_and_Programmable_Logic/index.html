Chapter Overview

Semiconductor memories are essential components of computers, microcontrollers, embedded systems and digital electronic devices. They store program instructions, configuration data and application data.

This chapter introduces RAM, ROM, Flash memory, memory organization, address decoding and programmable logic devices such as PLA, PAL, CPLD and FPGA.

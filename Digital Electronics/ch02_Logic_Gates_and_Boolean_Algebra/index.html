Chapter 2: Logic Gates and Boolean Algebra

Digital Electronics – Placement-Oriented Course

Chapter Overview

This chapter covers fundamental logic gates, their truth tables, Boolean algebra operations and the implementation of digital circuits using universal gates.

These concepts form the foundation of combinational logic design, microprocessor hardware and digital circuit troubleshooting.

  • Answer common digital electronics interview questions.

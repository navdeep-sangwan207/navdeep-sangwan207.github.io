2. NAND Gate

A NAND gate is a combination of an AND gate followed by a NOT gate. It produces the complement of the AND operation.

The NAND gate output is LOW only when all its inputs are HIGH. For every other input combination, its output is HIGH.

Boolean Expression

Y = (A · B)̅

The bar over the complete expression indicates that the AND output is inverted.

Logic Symbol

The small circle, called a bubble, at the output represents logical inversion.

Truth Table of NAND Gate

A B AND Output NAND Output
0001
0101
1001
1110
Remember: NAND output is LOW only when all inputs are HIGH.

Applications of NAND Gate

  • Universal logic circuit implementation.
  • Latch and flip-flop circuits.
  • Digital control systems.
  • Memory circuits.
  • Logic signal processing.

3. NOR Gate

A NOR gate is a combination of an OR gate followed by a NOT gate. It produces the complement of the OR operation.

The NOR gate output is HIGH only when all its inputs are LOW.

Boolean Expression

Y = (A + B)̅

The bar over the complete expression indicates that the OR output is inverted.

Logic Symbol

The output bubble indicates inversion of the OR operation.

Truth Table of NOR Gate

A B OR Output NOR Output
0001
0110
1010
1110
Remember: NOR output is HIGH only when all inputs are LOW.

Applications of NOR Gate

  • Universal logic circuit implementation.
  • SR latch circuits.
  • Digital control systems.
  • Zero-detection circuits.
  • Logic signal processing.

Full Form NOT AND NOT OR

Universal Gate Yes Yes

5. Relationship with AND and OR Gates

NAND and NOR gates are obtained by inverting the outputs of AND and OR gates respectively.

NAND Relationship

AND output = A · B

NAND output = (A · B)̅

NOR Relationship

OR output = A + B

NOR output = (A + B)̅

Important: These relationships are closely connected to De Morgan's theorems, which will be studied in Lesson 7.

6. Common NAND and NOR Gate ICs

IC Number Function Gate Quantity
7400 NAND Gate Four 2-input NAND gates
7401 NAND Gate Four 2-input open-collector NAND gates
7402 NOR Gate Four 2-input NOR gates
74266 XNOR Gate Four 2-input XNOR gates
Practical note: IC 7400 and IC 7402 are common standard TTL devices. IC 7401 has open-collector outputs and requires appropriate external pull-up resistors. Always check the exact manufacturer's datasheet.

7. Practical Experiment: NAND Gate Using IC 7400

Objective

To verify the truth table of a two-input NAND gate using IC 7400.

Required Components

  • IC 7400
  • 5V regulated power supply
  • LED
  • 330Ω to 1kΩ current-limiting resistor
  • Two input switches or logic input sources
  • Breadboard and connecting wires

Connections for Gate 1 of IC 7400

IC Pin Connection
Pin 14 +5V supply
Pin 7 Ground
Pin 1 Input A
Pin 2 Input B
Pin 3 Output Y
LED connection: Connect the output through a suitable resistor to the LED and ground to observe a HIGH output. Use a current-limiting resistor and verify the IC's output-current limits. Ensure each input is driven to a valid logic level; do not leave inputs floating.

10. Lesson Summary

  • NAND is the inverted form of AND.
  • NOR is the inverted form of OR.
  • NAND output is LOW only when all inputs are HIGH.
  • NOR output is HIGH only when all inputs are LOW.
  • The small output bubble represents inversion.
  • NAND and NOR are universal gates.

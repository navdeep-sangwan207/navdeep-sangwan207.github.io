2. Introduction to Number System Conversion

Number system conversion is the process of representing the same numerical value in different number systems.

The four commonly used number systems are:

  • Binary – Base 2
  • Octal – Base 8
  • Decimal – Base 10
  • Hexadecimal – Base 16
Why are conversions important?
  • Computers process information using binary representation.
  • Engineers use decimal values for calculations.
  • Octal and hexadecimal provide compact representations of binary data.
  • Embedded programmers use hexadecimal values for registers and memory addresses.

3. Binary to Decimal Conversion

To convert binary into decimal, multiply each binary digit by its positional weight and add all the results.

Method:

Multiply each bit by the corresponding power of 2, starting from 20 at the rightmost position.

Example 1: Convert (101101)₂ into decimal.

Binary:       1    0    1    1    0    1
Weight:      32   16    8    4    2    1

\[ =1(32)+0(16)+1(8)+1(4)+0(2)+1(1) \]

\[ =32+0+8+4+0+1=45 \]

Answer: (101101)₂ = (45)₁₀

4. Decimal to Binary Conversion

To convert a positive decimal integer into binary, repeatedly divide the number by 2 and record the remainders.

Example 1: Convert (25)₁₀ into binary.

25 ÷ 2 = 12   Remainder 1
12 ÷ 2 = 6    Remainder 0
 6 ÷ 2 = 3    Remainder 0
 3 ÷ 2 = 1    Remainder 1
 1 ÷ 2 = 0    Remainder 1

Read the remainders from bottom to top:

Answer: (25)₁₀ = (11001)₂

Example 2: Convert (42)₁₀ into binary.

42 ÷ 2 = 21   Remainder 0
21 ÷ 2 = 10   Remainder 1
10 ÷ 2 = 5    Remainder 0
 5 ÷ 2 = 2    Remainder 1
 2 ÷ 2 = 1    Remainder 0
 1 ÷ 2 = 0    Remainder 1
Answer: (42)₁₀ = (101010)₂

5. Binary to Octal Conversion

To convert binary into octal:

  1. Start grouping binary digits from the right.
  2. Make groups of three bits.
  3. Add leading zeros to the left if required.
  4. Convert each group into its octal equivalent.

Example: Convert (1101011)₂ into octal.

Step 1: Group into three bits from the right.

Binary:       1  1  0  1  0  1  1

Groups:      001  101  011

Step 2: Convert each group into octal.

001 = 1
101 = 5
011 = 3
Answer: (1101011)₂ = (153)₈

6. Octal to Binary Conversion

Each octal digit is replaced with its corresponding three-bit binary equivalent.

Example: Convert (572)₈ into binary.

5 = 101
7 = 111
2 = 010

Combine the binary groups:

Answer: (572)₈ = (101111010)₂

7. Binary to Hexadecimal Conversion

To convert binary into hexadecimal:

  1. Group binary digits into sets of four, starting from the right.
  2. Add leading zeros to the left if required.
  3. Convert each group into its hexadecimal equivalent.

Example: Convert (11010110)₂ into hexadecimal.

Binary:       1101  0110

1101 = D
0110 = 6
Answer: (11010110)₂ = (D6)₁₆

8. Hexadecimal to Binary Conversion

Each hexadecimal digit is replaced by its equivalent four-bit binary group.

Example: Convert (3A7)₁₆ into binary.

3 = 0011
A = 1010
7 = 0111

Combine the groups:

Answer: (3A7)₁₆ = (001110100111)₂

9. Decimal to Octal Conversion

Repeatedly divide the decimal number by 8 and record the remainders. Read the remainders from bottom to top.

Example: Convert (156)₁₀ into octal.

156 ÷ 8 = 19   Remainder 4
 19 ÷ 8 = 2    Remainder 3
  2 ÷ 8 = 0    Remainder 2
Answer: (156)₁₀ = (234)₈

10. Octal to Decimal Conversion

Multiply each octal digit by its positional weight based on powers of 8 and add the results.

Example: Convert (347)₈ into decimal.

\[ =3(8^2)+4(8^1)+7(8^0) \]

\[ =192+32+7=231 \]

Answer: (347)₈ = (231)₁₀

11. Decimal to Hexadecimal Conversion

Repeatedly divide the decimal number by 16 and record the remainders. Convert remainders 10 to 15 into A to F.

Example: Convert (254)₁₀ into hexadecimal.

254 ÷ 16 = 15   Remainder 14 (E)
 15 ÷ 16 = 0    Remainder 15 (F)

Read the remainders from bottom to top:

Answer: (254)₁₀ = (FE)₁₆

12. Hexadecimal to Decimal Conversion

Multiply each hexadecimal digit by its positional weight based on powers of 16 and add the results.

Example: Convert (2B4)₁₆ into decimal.

B = 11

\[ =2(16^2)+11(16^1)+4(16^0) \]

\[ =512+176+4=692 \]

Answer: (2B4)₁₆ = (692)₁₀

13. Octal to Hexadecimal and Hexadecimal to Octal

The simplest method is to convert the original number into binary first, then convert the binary number into the target number system.

13.1 Octal to Hexadecimal

Example: Convert (725)₈ into hexadecimal.

Step 1: Convert each octal digit into three binary bits.

7 = 111
2 = 010
5 = 101

Binary = 111010101

Step 2: Group into four bits from the right.

0001 1101 0101

Step 3: Convert each group into hexadecimal.

0001 = 1
1101 = D
0101 = 5
Answer: (725)₈ = (1D5)₁₆

13.2 Hexadecimal to Octal

Example: Convert (3B)₁₆ into octal.

Step 1: Convert hexadecimal digits into binary.

3 = 0011
B = 1011

Binary = 00111011

Step 2: Group into three bits from the right.

000 111 011

Step 3: Convert each group into octal.

000 = 0
111 = 7
011 = 3
Answer: (3B)₁₆ = (73)₈

17. Lesson Summary

  • Number system conversion represents the same value in different bases.
  • Binary-to-decimal conversion uses powers of 2.
  • Decimal-to-binary conversion uses repeated division by 2.
  • Binary-to-octal conversion groups bits in sets of three.
  • Binary-to-hexadecimal conversion groups bits in sets of four.
  • Octal-to-binary and hexadecimal-to-binary conversions use digit replacement.
  • Decimal-to-octal and decimal-to-hexadecimal conversions use repeated division by their respective bases.
  • Octal and hexadecimal can be converted through binary.

About This Course

This Digital Electronics course is designed for Electronics and Communication Engineering students and candidates preparing for technical interviews and placement opportunities.

It covers essential digital electronics concepts, including number systems, logic gates, Boolean algebra, combinational circuits, flip-flops, registers, counters, timing circuits, digital logic families, semiconductor memories, ADC and DAC.

The course also includes practical digital circuit testing, troubleshooting and simulation using Proteus and Logisim Evolution.

Course Learning Outcomes

After completing this course, you should be able to:

  • Perform number system conversions and binary arithmetic.
  • Understand and implement digital logic gates.
  • Simplify Boolean expressions using Karnaugh maps.
  • Design combinational logic circuits.
  • Understand flip-flops, registers and counters.
  • Analyze timing diagrams and finite state machines.
  • Understand TTL, CMOS and digital interfacing.
  • Explain semiconductor memory organization.
  • Understand ADC and DAC operation.
  • Test and troubleshoot basic digital electronic circuits.
  • Simulate digital circuits using suitable software.
